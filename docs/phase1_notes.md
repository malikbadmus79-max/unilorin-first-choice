# Phase 1 notes: first-choice applicant series

As of 30 October 2026.

## Sources

- `data/raw/applicants_sources.csv` holds 138 institution-year values from 17 published sources (S01–S17), copied as printed, with the outlet, date and attribution of each.
- `data/raw/funnel_sources.csv` holds 12 values on Post-UTME screening and admission at the University of Ilorin.
- JAMB's own policy-meeting documents (jamb.gov.ng/Speeches-Presentation/) could not be retrieved; all values come from news reports and university statements that cite JAMB.

## Rule

A value is accepted when at least two independent outlets print the same number for the same UTME year. A value with one source is kept but marked "single source". Conflicting values are recorded in `notes`.

On accepted values, first-choice applications fell from 103,582 in 2020 to 52,514 in 2026, a fall of 49.3%. The university's share of all applicants fell from 5.31% to 2.34%. Its rank fell from first (2018–2024) to third (2025) and seventh (2026).

## Problems found in published figures

1. 2022 is missing. JAMB's April 2024 statement says the University of Ilorin ranked first in 2022 but no outlet prints its 2022 figure. The 97,351 reported by the university for the "2022/2023 admission exercise" is a JAMB award category ("Most Subscribed Institution by Candidates"); the University of Lagos's figure in the same category (75,967) differs from its 2022 first-choice figure (61,606), so the two measures are not the same.
2. The BusinessDay five-year review (S17, 23 May 2026) appears shifted by one year: its 2026 value equals the 2025 lists and its 2022 value equals the 2021 lists.
3. Legit.ng (S14, 8 July 2025) labels the 2025 figures "2024 application data".
4. 2024: Legit.ng prints 63,143 in its list and 64,143 in its text; Nnamdi Azikiwe University appears as 55,495 (allAfrica) and 54,495 (Legit.ng).
5. 2023: the University of Lagos appears as 61,606 (ThisDay, BusinessDay) and 60,606 (Legit.ng citing Premium Times).

## Update: national totals and peer panel

- `data/raw/national_totals_sources.csv`: 9 national UTME totals (2020–2026) with their measure (registered, sat, results released). JAMB's published shares for 2025 (2.79%) and 2026 (2.34%) imply denominators of about 2.03m and 2.24m, which match the registered totals, so JAMB's shares use registered candidates as the base. Registered totals for 2021, 2022 and 2023 are still missing.
- `data/interim/peer_panel.csv`: 63 institution-year values (50 confirmed by two or more outlets, 13 single-source).
- Of the seven universities in JAMB's top 10 in both 2021 and 2026, the University of Ilorin is the only one whose first-choice applications fell: 78,466 to 52,514 (−33%). The others rose by 5% (FUOYE) to 42% (OAU). Universities that left the top 10 (ABU, Bayero, Jos) have no published 2026 figure, so the comparison covers only universities that stayed in it (figures/fig1_change_2021_2026.png).
- Over the same period JAMB's registered candidates grew; exact growth from 2021 cannot be computed until the 2021 registered total is found.
